# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## 1. Shipping Options and Delivery Speeds
* **Standard Ground Shipping**: 3 to 5 business days across the continental US. Free on orders over $50.
* **Express 2-Day Shipping**: 2 business days for orders placed before 1:00 PM EST.
* **Overnight Delivery**: Next business day delivery available for select metropolitan areas.

## 2. Order Tracking
* As soon as an order is packed and dispatched, a shipping confirmation email containing a carrier tracking number (e.g. TRK123456) is sent to the customer.
* Tracking details can also be checked directly through our customer support assistant or online portal using your order number.

## 3. Damaged or Lost Shipments
* If a package arrives visibly damaged, please report it within 48 hours of delivery.
* For packages showing "Delivered" but not received, please allow 24 hours before contacting support for lost shipment investigations.
