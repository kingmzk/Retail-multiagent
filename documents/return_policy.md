# General Customer Return Policy

## 1. Overview
We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Most new, unopened, and unworn items purchased online can be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund to your original payment method.

## 2. General Return Requirements
* Items must be in original condition with all manufacturer tags, labels, and packaging.
* Proof of purchase (Order Number and confirmation email) is required for all returns.
* Final sale items, personalized goods, and gift cards are non-refundable.

## 3. How to Start a Return
1. Visit our online Returns Portal or contact Customer Support.
2. Enter your Order Number (e.g. #45231) and email address.
3. Print the prepaid return shipping label and attach it to your package.
4. Drop off the package at any authorized carrier drop-off location.

## 4. Refund Timeline
* Once your return is received and inspected at our distribution center (typically 2-4 business days), your refund will be processed.
* Refunds generally take **3 to 5 business days** to appear on your bank or credit card statement depending on your financial institution.
