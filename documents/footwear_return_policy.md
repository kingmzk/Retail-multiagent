# Footwear Return and Exchange Policy

## 1. 30-Day Return Window
Shoes, sneakers, and boots may be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

## 2. Condition Requirements
To be eligible for a return or refund:
* **Unworn Condition**: Shoes must be unworn, undamaged, and free of visible scuffs or outdoor dirt on the outsoles.
* **Original Packaging**: Footwear must be returned in the original branded shoe box, placed inside a protective shipping outer box.
* **Tags Attached**: All original product tags, stickers, and shoe laces must remain intact and attached.

## 3. Fit and Sizing Exchanges
* If the shoes do not fit comfortably, customers are entitled to a **free sizing exchange** for an alternate size in the same model, subject to inventory availability.
* We recommend trying shoes on clean, carpeted surfaces indoors to test fit before wearing outdoors.

## 4. Worn Footwear Exceptions
* Footwear showing signs of outdoor wear, creased uppers, or worn outsoles cannot be accepted for a standard return or refund.
* If worn footwear has a manufacturing defect, it is covered under our 1-Year Footwear Limited Warranty.
