# Retail Support Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. How do I track my order?
You can track your order by providing your Order Number (e.g. #45231) to our support assistant or entering your tracking number on the carrier's website.

## 2. Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?
Address changes can only be accommodated while an order is in **PROCESSING** status. Once an order status is updated to **SHIPPED**, the delivery address cannot be changed and package rerouting must be requested directly through the carrier using the tracking number.

## 3. Can I cancel my order?
Orders may be cancelled free of charge if they have not yet entered fulfillment. If the order has already shipped, please wait for delivery and initiate a standard 30-day return.

## 4. How do I contact human support?
If your inquiry requires personal assistance or escalations not handled by automated systems (such as billing disputes or fraud verification), our human support team is available Monday through Friday 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM EST.
