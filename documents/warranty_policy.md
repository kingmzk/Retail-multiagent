# Limited Product Warranty Policy

## 1. Scope of Coverage
* We provide a **1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty** on all footwear, outerwear, and electronics purchased through our official channels.
* This warranty covers defects in materials and craftsmanship, such as delaminating soles, failed seams, broken zippers, or hardware malfunctions under normal intended use.

## 2. Exclusions
* Normal wear and tear, cosmetic scuffs, improper care, accidents, or unauthorized modifications are not covered under warranty.

## 3. Warranty Claim Process
* To initiate a warranty evaluation, provide your original Order Number, photos of the defect, and a brief description of the issue.
* Approved warranty claims will receive a replacement item or store credit equal to the purchase price.
